HireFlow AI · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026. HireFlow AI is a student MVP built for the course Technology Entrepreneurship (group BDA-2405, Abylaikhan Torekhan and Aibyn Zhanashev). It helps small cafés hire staff through WhatsApp.

What we collect

When you write to the HireFlow WhatsApp number we store your WhatsApp number, the name in your WhatsApp profile, your answers to the vacancy questions (available days, whether pay and workload suit you, how long you plan to work, your name and a short description of experience), and the messages in this conversation. If you are hired, we store your answers to short check-ins on days 7, 30 and 90.

How we use it

Only to show your application to the café that posted the vacancy, to send you invitations, reminders and check-ins, and to measure how well the service works. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

The owner of the café you applied to and the two members of the HireFlow team.

How long we keep it

Until the end of the pilot (December 2026), after which the data is deleted.

Deleting your data

Write "delete my data" to the HireFlow WhatsApp number, or ask the team, and we will delete your messages and answers within 7 days.
